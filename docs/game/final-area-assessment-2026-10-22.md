## Assessment method

An A means the area's stated implementation and visual acceptance criteria are
met by inspected production captures and passing behavioral/content checks,
with no unresolved actionable defect found in that scope. It does not mean
perfect aesthetics, proven enjoyment, hardware certification or human-tested
accessibility. The plan defines that scope explicitly.
