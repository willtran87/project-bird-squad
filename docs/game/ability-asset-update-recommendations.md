# Ability Asset Update Recommendations

This pass wires new mechanical roles into existing runtime assets. The current art still works, but these are the best candidates for future visual refreshes because their mechanics now imply more specific props, poses, or encounter reads.

## Cards

- `swords_04` / Sheathed Quills: now strips enemy Cover before cycling the hand. Art would benefit from a visible sheathed blade/quill hook, broken guard panel, or defensive stance turned into a precise disarm.
- `swords_08` / Quill Ring: now punishes covered targets with piercing damage. Art can emphasize a circling pressure ring, tight tactical footwork, or a visual line that slips past armor/cover.
- `swords_elder` / Quill Elder: now gains a high-end piercing payoff against covered enemies. Art should read as veteran precision: an angle through shields rather than just a heavy strike.

## Waymarks

- `cache_hook`: now makes Caches offer one extra reward choice. Icon/art should emphasize a hook lifting multiple cache lids or tagged bundles.
- `roofline_compass`: now makes Caches offer two extra reward choices. Icon/art should read as a rare route-planning tool, not just Scrap economy.
- `sky_safe_harness`: now gives boss-fight Cover. Icon/art should look reinforced and boss-ready, with straps/plates that imply absorbing a major hit.
- `reopened_roofline`: now grants a free Preen at the next district start. Icon/art should show an opened route sign plus repair/preening marks, not only navigation.

## Enemies

- `gutter_baron`: Panel Guard now taxes Wingbeat when the flock has Cover. Art/readability could emphasize the Baron leaning into a barricade, forcing the flock to lose tempo.
- `tar_crowned_crow`: Chokepoint Call now taxes Wingbeat against covered play. Art/readability should show command presence and route control, not only raw damage.

## Future Additions

The next strongest mechanic candidates are:

- A visible Cache choice panel that can show all extra choices granted by cache-control Waymarks.
- One more Molt-specific reward card or Waymark that triggers when entering Molt.
- A healer/repair enemy in Maps 2-3 that forces priority targeting without relying on raw damage scaling.
