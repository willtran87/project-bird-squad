# Coherent City direction and review sheets

Companion to the art bible, not a replacement for its anatomy or identity rules.
Generate the direct-reference gallery with `node tools/build-coherency-gallery.mjs`.
It pairs the five Leaders' original views, the five residents, all four districts,
and all 110 approved card thumbnails/portraits. It reads canonical assets without
resampling or rewriting them. Open `.artifacts/coherent-city/reference.html`.

## Character sheets

The gallery copies each Leader's species and locked anchors from the art bible
instead of maintaining a second description. Front and rear views are compared
together. Reference the resident anatomy correction for Marn, Ivo, Oren and
Caldra; Sella retains the approved v2 cutout. Caldra is the peafowl patron and
the jackdaw Captain remains a different character. Cards use the species named
in canonical card data, even when a different bird shares its gameplay role.

Review silhouette, face/beak, plumage, clothing, carried objects, facing, feet,
scale and alpha. Runtime animation must keep the same original texture and
bounded transform. Never approve an atlas based only on dimensions or tests.

## Effect vocabulary and ownership

| Event | Primary explanation | Supporting cue | What yields |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Select | Owned card, target and forecast | Meter preview; explicit estimate reason | Old damage numbers and previous result |
| Commit | One canonical card illustration | One attached seal and bounded movement | Objective/coaching/history and ambient weather |
| Hit | Target contact and actual damage | Single number lane; synced contact sound | Extra supporting bursts and ambient sound |
| Protect | Cover interception and actual prevented damage | Roof/brace mark; block sound | A second shield/character body |
| Recover | Actual healing or returned resource | Landing/recovery mark | A new permanent HUD panel |
| Disrupt | Changed Tell/state and written interruption | Existing target feedback | Generic spell explosion |
| Surge/phase/victory | The consequential state change | Existing authored signature accent | Routine decorative spectacle |
| Settle | Actual resolved result, then idle | History keeps the explanation | Every retired cue, timer and pose |

Action-priority opacity belongs to an outer weather container; its inner
container alone owns ambient movement. Pausing freezes action timing. Reduced
motion retains the same written/shape cues without travel. Sound is never the
only explanation, and no mechanic requires musical timing.

## Surface consistency matrix

| Surface | Primary question | Context retained | Commitment / exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Title / Continue | What flight am I returning to? | Leader, district, pending task, chosen plan | Continue; settings/guide remain separate |
| Practice briefing | What will I try and why? | Selected lesson; original art; no saved changes | Start lesson / Back to exact lesson |
| District contract | Where am I and what matters here? | District premise and tactical question | One explicit contract choice |
| Route | Which gain and risk fit my flock? | Current node, selection, resources | Inspect / Take route |
| Combat intro | What threatens me; how do I win? | District, encounter, complete special rule | Read or dismiss; no free attack |
| Card selection | What changes if I play this? | Card, target, Wingbeats, protection, Roost comparison | Confirm / Cancel / Full rules |
| Combat result | What actually changed? | One resolved lane and causal history | Next intentional action |
| Market / reward / Preen | What am I giving up and receiving? | Exact choice, before/after rules, reading position | Inspect, select, confirm; explicit skip/back |
| Journal | What happened and what can I do? | Saved resident promises, current district | Scroll/read; explicit optional commitment |
| Outcome | Why did this flight end; what next? | Observed contributions, resident consequences | Rematch practice / next flight / menu |

Use the existing quiet controls and measured typography. Preserve minimum-size
readability, actual input hit areas, controller focus, remapped keys and spoken
summaries. Async artwork may fill a visual slot; it must not reset a choice or
replace a control after input registration. Text overflow goes to the existing
reader rather than a smaller illegible font or another overlay.

## November 14 source-art review

Reviewed the five front/rear Leader pairs, five resident cutouts, four route /
battle plate pairs, and all 110 approved cards in fourteen native gallery
captures (`cards-0` through `cards-104`). The gallery uses actual runtime files;
it does not substitute generated concepts. No replacement character art was
needed in this pass. The approved species silhouettes, bird-fitted clothing,
beaks, feet and facing remain the reference for runtime motion.

At 72 × 108, broad silhouettes and suit frames survive; tiny feather details,
scene props and the baked title cannot be treated as readable game instructions.
The darker Moon, Plumes Matron, Basins Five and several Snags especially depend
on the separate hand name/rules and full-size inspection. Preserve these readers
and runtime labels rather than brightening or repainting canonical identities.
The ten-of-suit group scenes are card illustrations, not animation pose sheets.

The source review exposed a framing mismatch: High Roost's existing night plate
was described as clear dawn. The runtime weather now says “Before dawn”. The
restored-city ending can still resolve into dawn. Older card prompt packages
describe additional times of day; they do not authorize changing the current
district plates or replacing an approved character during a combat animation.

Live verification is recorded separately in the Coherent City acceptance ledger.
Source review alone does not establish clean action timing or human readability.
